About Kanyakumari

Where Three Seas Meet

Kanyakumari is the southernmost tip of India where the Arabian Sea, the Bay of Bengal, and the Indian Ocean meet. It is famous for its breathtaking sunrise and sunset views.

Cultural Landmarks

The town is known for the Vivekananda Rock Memorial, Thiruvalluvar Statue, and the historic Kumari Amman Temple.

Nature & Beaches

A paradise for nature lovers with cool sea breeze, colorful sands, and scenic beaches perfect for relaxation.
